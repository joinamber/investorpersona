These questions hide technical financial logic behind intuitive, personal lifestyle choices.

\* \*\*\[D\]\*\* I look at where the locals are queuing around the block before tourist blogs pick it up. \*(Lynch)\*

\#\#\# Q2: What's your relationship with your smartphone upgrade cycle?

\* \*\*\[A\]\*\* I keep the same phone until it physically stops turning on. If it ain't broke, don't pay to replace it. \*(Buffett / Templeton)\*  
\* \*\*\[B\]\*\* I pre-order the newest flagship model months in advance for the raw processing power. \*(Cathie Wood / Simons)\*  
\* \*\*\[C\]\*\* I buy a reliable, mid-tier phone on a structured contract with phone insurance included. \*(Dalio)\*  
\* \*\*\[D\]\*\* I look for refurbished premium models that give me 90% of the features at 50% of the cost. \*(Lynch)\*

\#\#\# Q3: You’re planning a 2-week vacation. What is your itinerary style?

\* \*\*\[A\]\*\* A detailed, data-driven spreadsheet with contingencies for traffic, weather, and venue delays. \*(Simons / Dalio)\*  
\* \*\*\[B\]\*\* One-way ticket to an unconventional destination most people are afraid to travel to right now. \*(Templeton)\*  
\* \*\*\[C\]\*\* Renting a quiet cabin in the countryside with no cell service and a stack of books. \*(Buffett)\*  
\* \*\*\[D\]\*\* A high-energy tour through tech hubs, modern art galleries, and emerging cultural hotspots. \*(Cathie Wood)\*

\#\#\# Q4: How do you react when a viral internet debate breaks out online?

\* \*\*\[A\]\*\* Ignore it completely. Modern media is 99% noise designed to steal my attention. \*(Buffett / Simons)\*  
\* \*\*\[B\]\*\* Dive right into the comments to understand the societal shift happening beneath the surface. \*(Cathie Wood / Lynch)\*  
\* \*\*\[C\]\*\* Analyze both sides objectively to understand the structural incentives driving each camp. \*(Dalio)\*  
\* \*\*\[D\]\*\* Take the counter-intuitive position that everyone is reacting to emotionally. \*(Templeton)\*

\#\#\# Q5: What is your primary motivation for building wealth?

\* \*\*\[A\]\*\* Complete independence and peace of mind—never having to answer to anyone. \*(Buffett / Dalio)\*  
\* \*\*\[B\]\*\* Funding and building the future I want to live in. \*(Cathie Wood)\*  
\* \*\*\[C\]\*\* Solving a complex puzzle and optimizing my personal performance score. \*(Simons)\*  
\* \*\*\[D\]\*\* Spotting opportunities that others miss and proving my intuition right. \*(Lynch)\*

\#\# 4\. Why This Hook Drives High Viral Conversion

1\. \*\*Self-Identification Friction Reducer:\*\* Users love comparing themselves to famous personalities (Myers-Briggs / Horoscope effect).  
2\. \*\*Built-in Shareability:\*\* "I got an 88% match with Warren Buffett, who are you?" is an extremely natural share-to-story moment for Instagram or LinkedIn.  
3\. \*\*Instant Educational Bridge:\*\* Connects abstract financial theory (e.g., value factor investing) with a recognizable real-world figure people already trust.
